    WELCOME TO YOUR CHORE ERA

    Make progress
    while you play

    When you’re unsure where to start, let the claw help you choose.

    01 / LOAD

    Empty your head.

    Add specific chores, such as watering the plants, and set each one to Priority or Lower priority. You can change this later using the button beside each chore.

    02 / GRAB

    Let the claw decide.

    Use the arrow keys, hold the arrow buttons, or drag across the glass to aim, then press Space or “Let’s grab” to drop. Priority chores have three entries in the capsule pool instead of one, so they appear more often; each round displays up to nine shuffled entries.

    03 / CELEBRATE

    Claim your little win.

    Do your chore, mark it done, and watch your wins grow. You can also check chores off directly in the pile. On its first completion, a priority chore earns 30 coins and a lower-priority chore earns 10. Spend your coins on themes and capsule colors in the prize shop.

    Complete a chore each day to grow your streak and reach weekly milestones. The activity tracker uses your local calendar day; completing more chores on the same day does not add extra streak days. Your chores, streak, priorities, and wins are saved in this browser without needing an account.
